# Portrait credits

The stock portraits offered in the voice booth are crops of public-domain works from
[The Metropolitan Museum of Art's Open Access collection](https://www.metmuseum.org/about-the-met/policies-and-documents/open-access) (CC0).
They stand in until each leader has a portrait of their own.
