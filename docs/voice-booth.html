Voice Booth

Hearth & Empire, a 3D strategy game of rival nations — become the voice of one nation's leader.

You have been asked to become a leader in the game. Pick one below, then work through three steps. You can stop after any of them and send what you have; the voice can come later, or never.

  1. Who they are: a name, a title and a portrait. A couple of minutes.
  2. How they rule: eight questions that decide how the computer plays your nation. A couple of minutes.
  3. Their words and voice: rewrite any line, and record them with your microphone. About fifteen minutes.

Whenever you stop, press Download and send that one file back to whoever invited you.

Nothing is uploaded anywhere. Your work stays in this browser tab until you download it, and is lost if you close the tab first.

Step 2 · How they rule

How do they rule?

Answer as your leader would. When you are not the one playing this nation, the computer plays it the way you answer: how readily it goes to war, whether its towns turn to the church or the tavern, whether it walls itself in or rides out, and how it bargains. Skip any question, or all of them, to leave the nation as it was written.

You can stop here. Steps 1 and 2 are enough for your leader to appear in the campaign as you made them, with their lines shown as text. Download and send the file now, and come back to add your voice whenever you like: open this page again and choose Continue from a saved file.

Step 3 · Their words and voice (optional)

How they were written (play them your own way if you prefer):


Tips: a quiet room, the microphone a hand's width from your mouth, and leave half a second of silence before you speak. Each line should run two to six seconds. Record as many or as few as you like; unrecorded lines stay as text in the game. Each box says when the leader speaks the line: keep your own version to that moment, and to one or two sentences.
