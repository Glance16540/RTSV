Voice Booth

Hearth & Empire — become the voice of a nation's leader.

You have been asked to voice a leader in the game. It takes about fifteen minutes.

  1. Pick your leader below.
  2. Read each line aloud and record it. You can listen back and redo any line.
  3. Press Download my recordings and send that one file back to whoever invited you.
Nothing is uploaded anywhere. Your recordings stay in this browser tab until you download them, and are lost if you close it first.

Choose your leader

How to play them:


Tips: a quiet room, the microphone a hand's width from your mouth, and leave half a second of silence before you speak. Each line should run two to six seconds. Record as many or as few as you like; unrecorded lines stay as text in the game.
