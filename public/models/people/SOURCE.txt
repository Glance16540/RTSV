human.json (a glTF file with its data embedded): the mannequin and a selection of motions from the Universal Animation Library (standard edition) by Quaternius, https://quaternius.com, released under CC0 1.0. Trimmed to the motions the game uses.

heads.json, head_m.webp, head_f.webp, eye.webp, hair_1.webp, hair_2.webp: the heads, eyes, eyebrows, beard and five hairstyles of the Universal Base Characters kit (standard edition) by Quaternius, https://quaternius.com, released under CC0 1.0. Cut out and repacked by scripts/heads.py; the skin pictures are divided by the face's average colour so the game can give them any skin colour, and the hair pictures are kept as light and shade only so it can give hair any colour.
