Rise and Ruin

Raise a people from the Stone Age to the Gunpowder Age, one family at a time.

Families

Villagers are men ♂ and women ♀. Train them at the Town Center, or build a House and move one of each in (right-click the house, or use Find a couple). Housed couples raise new villagers on their own, for half the food.

Morale & heroes

Houses near your Town Center harden its defenders. From the Bronze Age a Warlord inspires nearby troops.

Children & calamities

Newborns are children: they must grow up before they can work. In the Iron Age, Prophets spend faith to unleash Plague and Earthquake.

Goal

Advance through five ages and bring down your rivals' Town Centers: a people with none, who do not begin another within a minute and a half, are beaten. With several rivals, they fight each other too.
